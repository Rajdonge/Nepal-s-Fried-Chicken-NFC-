Privacy Policy

Your privacy and safety are our top priorities

At Hello Bajar, accessible through hellobajar.com, we are committed to protecting your personal information. This Privacy Policy outlines how we collect, use, and safeguard your data when you use our website or mobile application.

1. Information We Collect

We may collect the following types of information to enhance your shopping experience:

  • Your name, email address, phone number, and delivery address
  • Account login details
  • Order, billing, and payment information
  • Device data such as IP address, browser type, and usage patterns

2. How We Use Your Information

Your data helps us to:

  • Process and deliver orders quickly and accurately
  • Enhance and personalize your shopping experience
  • Send order updates, notifications, and important alerts
  • Support customer queries and resolve issues effectively

3. Cookies & Tracking Technologies

We use cookies to improve website functionality, store preferences, and analyze traffic. You may disable cookies in your browser settings if you prefer.

4. Sharing Your Information

We respect your privacy and never sell your data. We only share information with:

  • Delivery providers to complete your orders
  • Secure payment gateways
  • Government or legal authorities when required by law

5. Data Security

We implement advanced security protocols and encrypted systems to protect your personal data from unauthorized access or misuse.

6. Your Rights

You have the right to:

  • Access and update your personal details
  • Request deletion of your account or data
  • Opt out of marketing notifications

7. Updates to This Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy periodically. All revisions will be posted on this page with the updated date.

8. Contact Us

If you have questions or requests related to our Privacy Policy, reach us at:

support@hellobajar.com
